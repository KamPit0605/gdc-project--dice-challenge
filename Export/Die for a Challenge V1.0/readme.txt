(Supposed to be a project for GDC's Roll of the Dice game jam, but got shelved due to time constraints, until now)

Die for a Challenge. A 5-minute RNG endless dice action-survival game. Can you survive for 5 minutes?

---Controls---

WASD - Move

F or LMB - Attack

Z or MMB - Auto-attack*

-Console controller is supported-

L-stick - Move

R-trigger or R-faceButton - Attack

R-stick-Button - Auto-attack*

*: Auto-attacking is a bit finicky. Not recommended to rely only on it.

---Important---

Certain sound effects may be loud. Be sure to adjust it, by the speaker icon, to your preference at the bottom right in the main menu or on pause 

Extended sources in the " SFX from" credits can't be shown if changing the main menu scene from the game scene.
